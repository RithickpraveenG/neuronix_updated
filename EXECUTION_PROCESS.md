## Module Explanation

### 1. Authentication Module
Handles registration, login, role-based access, and session security.

### 2. Symptom Intake Module
Captures symptoms and contextual information from the patient.

### 3. Report Upload Module
Accepts PDF, image, or text-based reports and stores them securely.

### 4. OCR Module
Extracts structured text from medical documents.

### 5. NLP Module
Understands clinical language and extracts entities, conditions, and symptoms.

### 6. AI Prediction Module
Uses trained models for probable conditions and urgency risk scores.

### 7. Clinical Decision Support Module
Provides explainable, doctor-reviewed recommendations.

### 8. Doctor Review Module
Lets doctors validate AI suggestions and prescribe follow-up actions.

### 9. Notification Module
Sends appointment reminders, prescription reminders, and safety alerts.

### 10. Data History Module
Stores health timeline events for continuity of care.
