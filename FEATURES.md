- Login and registration
- Profile management
- AI symptom checker
- Medical report upload
- AI analysis and health insights
- Health timeline and history
- Appointment booking
- Medication reminder
- Push notifications

## Doctor Module

- Secure login
- Doctor dashboard
- Patient record review
- AI decision support
- Report viewer
- Prescription generation
- Follow-up scheduling

## Admin Module

- Manage doctors
- Manage patients
- Analytics dashboard
- System-wide reporting
