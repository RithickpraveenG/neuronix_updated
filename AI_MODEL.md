# AI Module

## TensorFlow

TensorFlow is used to build and train deep learning models for clinical decision support, risk prediction, and structured insight generation.

## Scikit-learn

Scikit-learn is used for baseline classification, symptom-based disease prediction, and risk scoring with interpretable models.

## spaCy

spaCy is used for natural language processing of symptom descriptions, clinical notes, and user-entered health text.

## Tesseract OCR

Tesseract OCR extracts text from scanned reports, lab documents, and handwritten or printed medical files.

## Clinical Decision Support

The system generates evidence-based suggestions for doctors to review, including probable conditions, urgency levels, and next-step recommendations.

## Disease Prediction

The disease prediction module uses symptoms, patient context, and historical patterns to estimate likely conditions.

## Risk Analysis

Risk analysis evaluates urgency, severity probability, and follow-up priority for improved triage.

## Medical Report Interpretation

AI summarizes extracted report content into patient-friendly and clinician-friendly insights.

## NLP

NLP enables symptom understanding, entity extraction, and simplification of complex medical language.

## OCR

OCR enables the platform to ingest visual medical documents without manual transcription.
