# Project Overview

## Problem Statement

Healthcare systems often struggle to provide timely, understandable, and personalized guidance to patients while allowing clinicians to review cases efficiently. Patients may not understand symptoms, lab values, or next steps, and doctors often need faster tools to triage and support complex decision-making.

## Existing Challenges

- Fragmented patient information across systems
- Long wait times for interpretation of reports
- Limited patient education and health literacy support
- High clinician workload and burnout
- Inconsistent follow-up and medication adherence

## Proposed Solution

Neuronix combines AI-assisted analysis, secure clinical workflows, and a modern patient experience to provide:

- symptom triage support
- medical report interpretation
- risk-based recommendations
- doctor review workflows
- patient-friendly explanations of medical findings

## Objectives

- Assist clinicians without replacing human judgment
- Improve patient understanding of medical information
- Provide explainable AI outputs
- Create a scalable MVP for real-world deployment

## Target Users

- Patients seeking guidance and monitoring
- Doctors and specialists reviewing cases
- Administrators managing care operations

## Expected Outcomes

- Faster triage and better patient engagement
- Improved understanding of health information
- Better coordination between patients and providers
- A scalable foundation for future AI healthcare products
