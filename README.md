# Neuronix

Neuronix is an AI-assisted healthcare platform designed to support both patients and clinicians with safe, explainable, and privacy-conscious decision support.

## Vision

The platform helps patients understand symptoms and lab reports while giving doctors a structured clinical decision support layer for triage, follow-up planning, and care coordination.

## Project Scope

- Patient-facing symptom analysis and health insights
- Doctor-facing patient record review and decision support
- Admin analytics and operational oversight
- Secure storage of health data and documents
